About Me

Get to know me

Hi there! I'm Karim, a game engine and graphics programmer. Since January 2025 I've been working on PIX3D Engine, where I build its Vulkan and OpenGL renderer: grass, frosted glass, bloom, image-based lighting, materials and descriptors.

At Gnosis Labs I spent over three years building Virtual Lab with Unity and C#: interactive 3D science labs for schools. On the side I like building things from scratch, like a 2D physics engine, a C graphics framework and a Wolfenstein-style raycaster.

Today I'm in ITI's Full-Stack Web & AI track, I work on qodrat.ai, an AI exam-prep platform, and I teach engines, physics and design patterns on my Arabic YouTube channel. Let's build something real-time together!

What I do

My Services

Game Engine Development

From the core loop to the editor, I build engine systems in modern C++: entity-component architectures with EnTT, asset pipelines with Assimp, and ImGui tools that keep creators fast.

Graphics Programming

From the first triangle to the final frame, I write Vulkan and OpenGL renderers: PBR materials, image-based lighting, bloom and grass, all tuned to stay inside the frame budget.

Physics & Simulation

From collision detection to rigid bodies, I write physics systems from scratch, like my 2D physics engine physac, plus spatial structures like quadtrees that keep big simulations fast.

Unity Game Development

From prototype to release, I build Unity games and simulations in C#. At Gnosis Labs I made Virtual Lab, interactive 3D science labs for schools, with custom shaders and careful performance profiling.

Full-Stack Web & AI

From database to interface, I build web apps with AI inside them. I work on qodrat.ai, an AI exam-prep platform, and I'm growing my full-stack skills at ITI.
